# Product vision
Athar is an Arabic-first, free Islamic learning and private worship companion. The native Android app requires verified authentication. The existing public website remains usable, and administration becomes a separate web application backed by the same PHP services and database. English is a complete alternative language, not a best-effort text overlay.

Priorities are correctness, useful source-attributed content, polished accessible interactions, measured performance and future extension. No advertising, subscriptions, public worship rankings, paid inference or fabricated religious content. Night Prayer supplements Quran reading and khatmah; it never infers performed worship from playback. Full scope and acceptance gates are in the two approved requirement documents.
