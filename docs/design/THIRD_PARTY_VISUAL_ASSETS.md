## Hero references and original assets — planned

Task21.18 adds only the [original artwork/fallback specification](ARTWORK_LIBRARY.md) and planned [asset specification](HERO_ASSET_SPEC.json). No new visual resources, stock images or reference-image copies are added. User inspiration images do not establish portrait identity, artwork redistribution rights or canonical Quran text. Full Material Symbols and Noto font licenses ship under android/app/src/main/assets/licenses. Source SVGs and generated native paths are checked into the repository, so builds do not call icon/font websites. Scripts are maintenance tools, not runtime dependencies.

LottieFiles and Rive were researched. No specific third-party animation has been selected, downloaded or licensed; neither runtime is required. A future asset entry must record its specific asset URL, author, license, free status, app-use permission and permitted changes before import. No blanket claim is made about assets returned by search.
